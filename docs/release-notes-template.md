## 📥 What to download

Pick the file for your device from *Assets* below:

| Your device | Download |
|---|---|
| Almost every phone and tablet | **`kotohori-vVERSION-arm64.apk`** |
| Older or budget phones, if the one above says "not compatible" | `kotohori-vVERSION-armv7.apk` |
| Emulators | `kotohori-vVERSION-x86_64.apk` |
